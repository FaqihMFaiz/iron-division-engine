READ FIRST

Welcome to Iron Division — a division-level combined-arms operational combat simulator built for tabletop RPGs and historical wargaming. Instead of arcade hit-point trading, it simulates unit cohesion (Organization), armor piercing vs. hardness, realistic weapon engagement ranges, shared Supply Depots, dynamic weather & 24-hour lighting, and generates printable Form DA-774 After-Action Reports.

DRILL SERGEANT'S RECOMMENDATION:

New to Hearts of Iron or operational wargaming? Read the Field Manual ([FM] Manual) before you touch a single button. It breaks down every stat, every round phase, the Theatre Settings bar, and why your riflemen can't snipe across two kilometers — written in plain English by a Drill Sergeant.
